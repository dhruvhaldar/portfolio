> _Conducted full-scale aerodynamic benchmarking of the Mercedes DrivAer Notchback automotive reference model at $Re = 11.4 \times 10^6$ (AutoCFD Case 2) in a virtual wind tunnel domain ($120\text{ m} \times 44\text{ m} \times 20.33\text{ m}$) with moving ground boundaries, cross-validating Entropic LBM against Ford Pininfarina wind tunnel data, Hupertz experimental benchmarks, and ANSYS Discovery, while compressing 101 GB transient 3D simulation volumes to 0.6 GB (99.4% reduction)._

## Project Overview & Industrial Context

The **DrivAer model**, jointly developed by the Technical University of Munich (TUM) with Audi AG and BMW Group, bridges the gap between generic bluff bodies (such as the Ahmed body) and complex production passenger vehicles. Featuring realistic underbody styling, engine bay geometry, mirrors, and interchangeable rear upper structures (Notchback, Fastback, Estate), it serves as the global benchmark for passenger car aerodynamics.

In collaboration with **Mercedes**, this project evaluated the aerodynamic performance, wake vortex dynamics, and surface pressure distributions of the DrivAer Notchback configuration under high-speed cruise conditions. Simulations were performed using SankhyaSutra's proprietary **Entropic Lattice Boltzmann Method (LBM) solver (*Taral*)**, rigorously validated against **Ford Pininfarina** wind tunnel surface pressure probes, **Hupertz experimental benchmarks**, the **AutoCFD 2nd Workshop Case 2** international database, and commercial Navier-Stokes solvers (**ANSYS Discovery / Fluent**).

## Technical Challenges & Objectives

1. **Complex Separated Wake & Vortex Coherence:** Resolving high-Reynolds unsteady shear layers, A-pillar vortex roll-up, side mirror shedding, C-pillar trailing vortices, and underbody diffuser flow separation without empirical RANS eddy-viscosity over-damping.
2. **AutoCFD Case 2 Virtual Wind Tunnel Modeling:** Simulating the full AutoCFD benchmark domain with moving ground boundaries, wheelhouse rotation, and no-slip floor conditions at $Re = 11.4 \times 10^6$.
3. **Underbody Momentum Loss Resolution:** Capturing the intricate flow structures and localized vortex generation beneath the chassis and central exhaust tunnel.
4. **Massive Data Handling:** Processing multi-gigabyte transient 3D flow volumes for interactive post-processing and engineering analysis without memory exhaustion.
5. **Multi-Source Experimental Cross-Validation:** Correlating Entropic LBM predictions directly with Ford Pininfarina centerline/off-centerline pressure taps, Hupertz force coefficients, and AutoCFD participant submissions.

## Computational Methodology & Physics Setup

### Entropic Lattice Boltzmann Method (LBM)
Traditional CFD methods solve the macroscopic Navier-Stokes conservation equations, relying heavily on empirical turbulence closures (such as $k$-$\epsilon$ or SST $k$-$\omega$) that often over-diffuse detached wake vortices. 

In this study, simulations were executed with **SankhyaSutra Taral**, which solves the discrete Boltzmann transport equation on a structured octree lattice:
- **LES/DNS-like Resolution:** The Entropic formulation enforces strict thermodynamic stability and H-theorem entropy growth, capturing transient turbulent eddies natively without empirical calibration.
- **Immersed Boundary Near-Wall Treatment:** Automatically resolves near-wall shear and boundary layers across curved automotive body panels and wheel assemblies via immersed boundary and lattice bounce-back formulations.

### Virtual Wind Tunnel Domain & Case 2 Setup

Simulations adhered to the **AutoCFD 2nd Workshop Case 2** benchmark specification:
- **Domain Size:** Total length $X_{WT} = 120\text{ m}$ ($X_{WT\_up} = 40\text{ m}$ upstream, $X_{WT\_down} = 80\text{ m}$ downstream), width $Y_{WT} = 44\text{ m}$ ($-22\text{ m}$ to $+22\text{ m}$), and height $Z_{WT} \approx 20.33\text{ m}$ ($-0.3305\text{ m}$ to $20\text{ m}$).
- **Blockage Ratio:** Under $1.4\%$, eliminating artificial boundary constraint accelerations.
- **Ground Condition:** Slip floor upstream transitioning to a no-slip ground at $x_{BL} = -2.339\text{ m}$.
- **Reynolds Number:** $Re = 11.4 \times 10^6$ based on vehicle length ($L = 4.61\text{ m}$).

### The Algorithm
- Developed an automated spatial downsampling algorithm that intelligently filters high-gradient shear layers and boundary cells while downsampling uniform freestream regions.
- Achieved a **99.4% reduction in file size** (compressing the dataset from **101 GB down to 0.6 GB**).
- Enabled instantaneous generation of longitudinal and transverse velocity vector slices, pressure contours, and stream ribbons without loss of coherent turbulent structures.

## Engineering Impact & Delivery

- **Mercedes Aerodynamic Insights:** Verified aerodynamic drag-reduction recommendations for Mercedes vehicle programs, identifying trailing vortex mitigation opportunities along the notchback rear deck and underbody diffuser.
- **AutoCFD 2nd Workshop Validation:** Demonstrated that Entropic LBM matches leading commercial Navier-Stokes and LES solvers within the global AutoCFD benchmark spread, establishing solver accuracy for production passenger cars.
- **Underbody Modeling Guidelines:** Established concrete resolution criteria ($\Delta x \le 3\text{ mm}$ with extended sampling times) for automotive underbody CFD workflows, preventing underprediction of underbody momentum loss.
- **Workflow Acceleration:** Cut client visualization turnaround time from hours to minutes using the custom volume downsampling pipeline.
- **Diagnostics Integration:** Integrated Plot-Over-Line spatial extraction and velocity vector projections into the Taral GUI in collaboration with Intelimek Systems, creating standard test suites for solver releases.
