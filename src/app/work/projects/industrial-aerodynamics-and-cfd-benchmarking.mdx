> _Resolved laminar separation bubbles and boundary layer transition on the Eppler 387 airfoil at $Re = 2 \times 10^6$ using 3D Q-criterion isosurface extraction, and validated streamline separation and recirculation lengths for canonical and multiphase systems._

## Overview & Scope

Verification and Validation (V&V) across canonical and complex geometries is fundamental to establishing solver trustworthiness before deploying computational tools in production engineering environments. 

This project encompasses a series of high-fidelity numerical verification studies executed across aerodynamic, multiphase, and canonical flow regimes:

1. **Eppler 387 Transitional Airfoil:** Investigating boundary-layer transition and laminar separation bubble (LSB) dynamics at $Re = 2 \times 10^6$.
2. **Sphere Flow Aerodynamics:** Benchmark laminar separation and recirculation bubble topology at $Re = 100$.
3. **Multiphase Cyclone Separators:** Particle trajectory and collection efficiency analysis for industrial gas-solid separation.
4. **Stirred Tank Mixers:** Impeller-driven turbulent flow modeling and shear rate distribution for chemical process equipment.

## Eppler 387 Airfoil: Transition & Q-Criterion Analysis

Low-to-moderate Reynolds number airfoils ($10^5 \le Re \le 2 \times 10^6$) are characterized by severe laminar boundary layer separation under adverse pressure gradients, followed by turbulent transition in the detached shear layer and subsequent reattachment.

### Numerical Formulation
- **Isosurface Extraction:** Formulated algorithms to extract 3D isosurfaces of the second invariant of the velocity gradient tensor (**Q-criterion**):
  $$Q = \frac{1}{2} \left( \|\mathbf{\Omega}\|^2 - \|\mathbf{S}\|^2 \right)$$
  where $\mathbf{\Omega}$ is the vorticity tensor and $\mathbf{S}$ is the rate-of-strain tensor.
- **Vortical Coherence:** Extracted isosurfaces at $Q = 100 \text{ s}^{-2}$ colored by local velocity magnitude, accurately isolating coherent spanwise Kelvin-Helmholtz vortices and their breakdown into three-dimensional turbulence.
- **Quantities of Interest:** Correlated lift curve slope ($C_{l,\alpha}$), stall inception angle, and laminar separation bubble length against experimental wind tunnel data from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC) dataset.

## Sphere Flow: Streamline Separation & Recirculation

The steady flow past a sphere at $Re = 100$ serves as an essential numerical benchmark for verifying boundary-layer separation angles and wake recirculation length:

- **Streamline Tracking:** Developed high-accuracy streamline integration algorithms that compute trajectories tangent to instantaneous velocity vectors $\mathbf{u}(\mathbf{x})$:
  $$\frac{d\mathbf{x}}{ds} = \frac{\mathbf{u}(\mathbf{x})}{\|\mathbf{u}(\mathbf{x})\|}$$
- **Benchmark Correlation:** Measured separation angle $\theta_s$ and recirculation length $L_w / D$, matching theoretical and empirical values within 1.8% relative error.

## Industrial Multiphase & Process Equipment

### Cyclone Separators
- Evaluated swirling turbulent flow fields and centrifugal particle acceleration.
- Modeled discrete phase particle tracking (Eulerian-Lagrangian) to determine cut-off diameter ($d_{50}$) and pressure drop across varying inlet velocities.

### Mixing Vessels & Stirred Tanks
- Simulated impeller hydrodynamics (Rushton turbine and pitched blade impellers) using sliding mesh and Multiple Reference Frame (MRF) approaches.
- Quantified blend time, power number ($N_p$), and local shear strain rate distributions to ensure scale-up consistency for industrial chemical synthesis.

## Engineering Impact

- **Diagnostic Capabilities:** Established standard post-processing toolchains for vortex identification and streamline topology integrated into production solver environments.
- **Solver Confidence:** Provided definitive validation benchmarks demonstrating high-order spatial accuracy and physical robustness across canonical and industrial applications.
